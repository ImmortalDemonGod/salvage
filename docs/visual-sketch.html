Team Ratateam · Underwater Jam · concept sketch, not art direction

What the merged design looks like on screen

Three greybox mockups of the game we agreed on: a fish exploring a fallen merfolk civilization, condition-based fights against regular enemies, and full limb targeting saved for bosses. Shapes and layout only. All real art, style, and character design belong to Glass_Goat; this is just the skeleton to hang it on.

SCREEN 1Exploration: the hub reef

from Marc's doc Zelda-style exploration in the hub reef: an NPC pointing you toward the story, memory fragments that each play a lost verse of it, a song-seal puzzle door, a current barrier that opens once you hold the Tide Relic, and a trench borrowing Glass_Goat's "don't swim low" idea. HP and stamina stay on screen so the combat resources feel like part of the world.

SCREEN 2Regular fight: conditions, not limb rigs

from Glass_Goat's doc Regular enemies stay light: skills inflict the condition directly (Silt Burst blinds, Fin Slash slows) and every action costs stamina. The refund comes from the merge: a landed disable returns some stamina. HP sits apart as the resource you protect, which is why Heal Song is marked rare. One enemy, one condition chip, one readable turn.

SCREEN 3Boss fight: full limb targeting

the merge Bosses are where the full body-part system earns its cost: four targetable parts with their own durability, and breaking the right one ends the phase. This is the "boss as puzzle" from Marc's doc, delivered as boss set-pieces (two in the jam budget) instead of rigged onto every enemy.
