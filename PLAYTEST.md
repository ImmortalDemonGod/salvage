# SALVAGE, for playtesters

**Play it here: https://salvage-chi.vercel.app/**

Keyboard or mouse. It runs about ten minutes. Nine beats, four fights, two
locks. There is no save; refreshing starts over.

## Controls

You can click everything, or use keys.

- **Click a station** to move the selected diver there. **Click it again**
  to attack from it. **Click a diver's card** to select that diver.
- Keys: `1` `2` `3` pick a diver. The letter printed on each station moves
  there. `SPACE` and `F` use that diver's two abilities. `ENTER` ends the
  turn, or moves the story on.
- At a lock, `1` `2` `3` `4` turn the valves and `ENTER` leaves when the
  way is open.

## What we most want to know

1. **Did you know what to do?** The last version's testers said they did
   not. If you are lost, say where exactly.
2. **Could you tell what the enemy was about to do?** Every attack is
   announced a turn ahead and drawn on the board. Did that read, or did
   you find out by being hit?
3. **Did moving ever feel worth it?** Standing somewhere nothing is
   announced costs you a turn of damage. Was that a real decision?
4. **Where were you bored?** Name the beat.
5. **What did you think the locks wanted from you?**

## Known, so you do not need to report it

- **All words are placeholder** and marked as such; Marc writes the real
  ones. The story is a skeleton, not a draft.
- **All art is placeholder**, drawn in code. Glass_Goat's characters and
  every real animation are still to come, so nothing is rigged and no
  enemy has a proper attack animation. What is there is a brief for that
  work, not an attempt at the finished look.
- The music is four procedural notes, not a score.
- There is no boss and no encounter whose layout changes mid-fight. The
  run stops after the dredge.
- The first fight is deliberately forgiving. If it feels too easy, that is
  measured and known: a casual player wins it about 90% of the time.
- Character names (`Prototype1`, `Proto5`) and two ability names
  (`Attck1`, `Attck2`) are working identifiers taken from the animation
  rig. They will not ship.

## If it breaks

Tell us the beat number from the top of the screen and what you pressed.
The build reports its own position, so that is enough to reproduce it.
